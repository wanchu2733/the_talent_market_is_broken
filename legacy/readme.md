THE CHALLENGE
The Talent Market Is Broken. AI Is Making It Worse.
Both employers and candidates are overwhelmed by a growing volume of AI-generated content, while access to trustworthy, real-time information remains limited.

From the Employer Perspective
AI-generated volume: Thousands of increasingly similar applications make meaningful evaluation harder.

Stale talent data: Profiles are outdated, incomplete or inactive.

Static evidence: CVs miss GitHub work, publications, certifications, learning and communities.

Rising pressure: Lean teams must identify qualified talent faster and at lower cost.

From the Candidate Perspective
Generic job ads: AI-generated descriptions blur meaningful differences between roles.

Limited transparency: Current insight into culture, growth and employer reality is scarce.

Fragmented signals: Reviews, business performance, innovation and technology sit across many sources.

Blind discovery: The right role is hidden in a sea of near-identical postings.
